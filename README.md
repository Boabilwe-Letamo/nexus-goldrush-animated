# Nexus Goldrush Animated

Create a website for Nexus Logistica. In terms of uploads, I have uploaded the company profile that should give you a direction in building the website. Secondly, is the logo as an original on transparent background. Then is the picture of the company CEO. Then next is a picture of the company CCO. Use the picture of the COO from the profile. I need the website to look as great as it possibly can, with a large number of moving 3D elements that will just keep the webpage alive. The color scheme is generally black and gold, refer to the gold in the logo.
